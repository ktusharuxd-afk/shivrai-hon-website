Help Centre

Everything you need to start mining and using HON.

What is SHIVRAI HON? ▼
SHIVRAI HON (HON) is an independent decentralized cryptocurrency. Every coin is earned through Proof of Work mining — no pre-mine, no ICO, no central authority.
How do I start mining HON? ▼
The easiest way is Web Mining — go to the Mine HON page, create an account, and click "Start Mining". Your browser will automatically mine HON. No downloads or setup needed!
How much HON do I earn per block? ▼
Web mining: 2000 Points per block (30 min). 2000 Points = 1 HON. Daily limit: 10,000 Points = 5 HON per day. Node mining: 2000 HON per block (8 hrs), with a 12-year reward cycle.
What is the block time? ▼
Target block time is 5 minutes, using the HONPoW algorithm which automatically adjusts difficulty to maintain this target.
Is web mining safe? ▼
Yes, completely safe. Mining happens directly in your browser — no downloads, no software needed. Your wallet and earnings are protected and never shared with anyone.
What is wHON? ▼
wHON is the tradeable version of HON. Once the bridge is live, you can convert your mined HON into wHON and trade it on crypto exchanges. Coming soon.
Can I mine on mobile? ▼
Yes! Open the mining page on any device — phone, tablet, or laptop. Login with Google. Hit Start Mining. That's it. Your HON starts accumulating immediately. No downloads. No setup. No excuses.
Where is my wallet stored? ▼
Your wallet is created automatically when you first login. It is linked to your Google account and secured. Your HON balance is always yours — no one can take it or freeze it.
1

Go to Mine HON page

Visit mining-dashboard.html or click "⛏ Mine HON" in the navigation bar from any page.

2

Create your account

Click "Continue with Google" to sign up instantly. Your HON wallet address is automatically generated when you first login.

3

Login to your account

Click "Continue with Google" — one click login. You'll be taken directly to the mining dashboard.

4

Click Start Mining

Hit the gold "⛏ Start Mining" button. Your browser connects to the HON network and begins mining. You'll see live logs in the mining console.

5

Earn 2000 Points per block

Every successful block gives you 2000 Points (30 min per block). 2000 Points = 1 HON. Daily limit: 10,000 Points (5 HON). Watch your balance grow in real-time!

6

Send HON to anyone

Go to "📤 Send HON" tab, enter the receiver's HON address (starts with bc1q...) and amount. Click Send!
